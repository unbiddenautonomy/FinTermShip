Four directions that change how FinTermShip works, not just how it's coloured

Why every version looked the same. The brief was "Duolingo-like", so the skeleton was copied first: a path of lessons, a five-tab bottom bar, streak and XP chips, rounded cards. Every revision after that only changed the paint (copper, green, soft colours, textures).

Why it reads as AI slop. Rounded cards everywhere, pill buttons, a warm cream background with a serif wordmark and one earthy accent is the default look AI tools produce. Nothing in it comes from finance or from interviews.

What these try instead. Each one starts from a metaphor out of the learner's real world and lets that decide navigation, interaction and type. Each is live: tap, answer, swipe.

Interview Room

The app is a conversation with a desk head who asks questions the way a real interviewer does, follows up, and pushes back.

The bet
Your users are prepping for interviews, so practise the actual interview. Rounds replace units, the interview date replaces the streak.
The risk
Needs follow-up questions written per term, and a free-text answer needs grading.

Recall Exchange

Every term is a listed stock whose price is how well you remember it. Prices decay daily; answering defends them.

The bet
Spaced repetition you can see. The scheduler you already have becomes the market, and the user learns to read a watchlist while they study.
The risk
Dense screens can feel like work. Needs care so it stays a game, not a terminal.

Term Lines

The syllabus is a metro map. Categories are lines, terms are stations, and terms people confuse are interchanges.

The bet
Uses the confusables data you already have to show how ideas connect. Lessons become rides of a few stops.
The risk
Hand-laying 400 terms on a map is real work; would need an auto-layout per zone.

Call It

One loud printed card, one claim, flick right for true or left for false. Built for 30 seconds in a queue.

The bet
Speed and feel over structure. Risograph two-ink print gives it a look nothing else in finance has.
The risk
True/false alone is shallow; it works as the quick mode beside one of the others.

These are samples only. Nothing in the app or repository was changed. The ideas also combine well: for example Interview Room as the main mode with Call It as the quick daily habit.
